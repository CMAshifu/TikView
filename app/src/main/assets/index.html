Use TikView on your phone

Phone and computer must be on the same Wi-Fi.
  1. Open the camera on your iPhone or Android and point it at the code. Tap the link that pops up.
  2. Or type in the phone's browser and enter this code:
  1. iPhone (Safari): tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
Keep TikView running on this computer while you use the phone app.

Follow streamers

Type a @username and tap +. A red ring and LIVE badge show who's live (checked every 90 seconds).

Watch, record and save

  • ▶ Watch plays a video. ● Watch live opens their live.
  • ● Record records a live; Auto-record lives records every time they go live.
  • ⬇ Save keeps a video. Find everything in the Downloads tab.

Discover new streamers

In the Discover tab, search a topic (like AI) or tap a category. TikTok shows the results; copy a profile or live link and paste it into TikView to follow them.

Share from TikTok

In the TikTok app, tap Share → TikView on any video, profile or live.

Use it on your phone

Click 📱 Connect phone and scan the code with your phone's camera.

Light or dark

The round button at the top switches between Auto, Light and Dark.

Be fair to creators

Downloads and recordings are for personal viewing. Don't re-upload other people's content without permission.
